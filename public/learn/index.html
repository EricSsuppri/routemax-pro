The math your delivery app does not show you

Take a $12 offer for 8 miles. The payout looks fine. The profit is a different story:

Payout: $12.00

Fuel: −$1.27  ·  Wear and tear: −$2.40

True net: $8.33

With an unpaid 4-mile ride back: $6.49. The ride back alone costs $1.84.

That is the number that matters. A bigger offer is not always the better offer — a smaller, closer job often pays more per hour once the real costs are counted.

Is a $12 DoorDash order for 8 miles worth it?

It depends on the real costs. A $12 payout for 8 miles leaves $8.33 after $1.27 of fuel and $2.40 of wear and tear. With an unpaid 4-mile ride back, you keep $6.49. RouteMax Pro does this math for you before you accept the offer.

How do I know if a delivery offer is worth it?

Compare the payout against the miles, the fuel, the wear on your car, your time, and the unpaid return trip. An offer that looks good on payout alone often pays less per hour than a smaller, closer offer.
